# Avago Technologies

> Avago Technologies Limited is a RETIRED corporate brand. It is not a subsidiary of Broadcom — it is
> Broadcom's legal predecessor and former name. On 1 February 2016 Avago Technologies Limited completed
> its $37 billion acquisition of Broadcom Corporation and renamed itself Broadcom Limited, today
> Broadcom Inc. (NASDAQ: AVGO). There is no Avago developer program, no Avago API, and no Avago
> documentation. If you are looking for an API, go to Broadcom.
